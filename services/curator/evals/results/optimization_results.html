What changed in each version

Cost is per article on the benchmark text unless noted

v1Baseline
  • Sonnet 4.6 wrote every field of every item: prompt, answer, alternates, context sentence and notes.
  • Cache keyed on the whole article, so any other article paid full price.
$0.208621,272 in · 9,653 out
v2Slim replies
  • Model replies only with {id, gloss, form?, alternates?, skip?}; code builds the citation form, article and context.
  • Compact JSON input; empty sentences left out.
  • Shared word-level translation cache replaced the per-article cache.
$0.0276Etiqueta article · 3,592 in · 1,124 out
v3Fewer rejects
  • Words are no longer rejected for a missing sentence; a window around the word is used instead.
  • English words filtered before the model call; same-spelling words cached as skips.
$0.02824,151 in · 1,048 out
v4Model switch
  • Haiku 5.5 as the main model, with automatic fallback to Sonnet 4.6 if a batch fails.
  • Prompt rewritten for the shared cache: general dictionary meaning, at most two senses.
$0.00125,910 in · 1,309 out
v5Any language
  • Language profiles for Spanish, English, Russian and a generic fallback for any spaCy language.
  • Prompt no longer assumes the learner speaks English.
$0.0013Russian · 5,146 in · 1,500 out
v6Readings
  • Russian stress marks moved to a separate Sonnet 4.6 pass, sent only multi-syllable words.
  • Chinese support with pinyin generated in code, and traditional text parsed as simplified.
$0.0013Chinese · 5,089 in · 1,506 out
